About

Fanglu Sun (孙芳露)

she/her · Suzhou · Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University

Deputy Head and Assistant Professor in the Department of International Studies at Xi’an Jiaotong-Liverpool University. An international relations scholar, she uses quantitative and data-driven methods to study Chinese foreign policy and the role of the Global South in international organizations.

Welcome to my blog. Join me as I use data to explore how the world works—and what the evidence can tell us about international politics.

Positions

  • Deputy Head, Department of International Studies, Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University
  • Assistant Professor, Department of International Studies, Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University, 2022–present
  • Director, MA IR Program, Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University, 2022–2023
  • Assistant Professor, School of International Relations and Public Affairs, Fudan University, 2017–2022
  • Director, Sciences Po–Fudan “Europe and Asia in International Affairs” Dual MA Program, 2017–2022

Education

  • Ph.D., Political Science, Rice University, 2016
  • M.A., Political Science, Rice University, 2012
  • M.A., Government, Nankai University, 2010
  • B.A., Government, Nankai University, 2008
