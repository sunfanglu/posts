Global South

Voices of the Global South: G77 solidarity in the General Assembly

Forthcoming · Fanglu Sun

Sun, Fanglu. Forthcoming. “Voices of the Global South. The Evolution of G77 Member’s Solidarity in the United Nations General Assembly.” International Journal of Arts, Humanities and Social Sciences 6(9).

The article sits in a longer project on how developing countries vote, speak, and coordinate inside the United Nations. Related work listed on the CV includes the National Social Science Fund project (2020–2025) on the institutional discursive power of developing countries, and the working papers “Cohesion or Cacophony: Developing Countries in the UN” and “Agendas, Divisions, and International Networks Exhibited in the United Nations Debates.”

Related talks

  • “Unity or Divergence: Rising Powers and Their Solidarity with the Global South in the United Nations General Assembly.” Duke Kunshan University, 2025.
  • “The Drastic Shift in the Discourse of the Global South in China: From Skepticism to Embrace.” ISA Virtual Conference, 2025.
  • “Agendas, Divisions, and International Networks Exhibited in the United Nations Debates.” Shanghai Jiao Tong University, 2024.
