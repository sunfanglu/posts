Chinese foreign policy

China’s perspective on world and regional orders

2019 · Fanglu Sun, with Steve Wood and Patricia Schneider

Sun, Fanglu, with Steve Wood and Patricia Schneider. 2019. “China’s Perspective on World and Regional Orders: Politics, Philosophy, Security.” Sicherheit und Frieden (Security and Peace) 37(3): 139–157.

The article is listed as SSCI and was funded by the DAAD Strategic Partnerships China Fund project “Australia, China, EU: Relations Dogged by Controversies” (2019). It belongs to the research line on how China describes world and regional order, alongside later writing on developing countries and international institutions.

A related talk the same year is “Chinese Leaders’ Visits Abroad and the Impacts on China’s International Economic Engagement,” given at the University of Hamburg and the University of Nottingham Ningbo.
