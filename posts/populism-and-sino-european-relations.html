Chinese foreign policy

The rise of populism and its impact on Sino-European relations

2021 · Fanglu Sun

Sun, Fanglu. 2021. “L’ascesa del populismo e il suo impatto sulle relazioni sino-europee (The Rise of Populism and Its Impacts on Sino-European Relations).” OrizzonteCina Quarterly: 22–31. In Italian.

The essay is paired with a non-public policy report written with Jasmin Hasic (Sarajevo School of Science and Technology): “The Rise of Populism in Europe and Its Implications for Europe–China Relations” (2020), funded by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of China under the Compass Plan (2019–2020).

From 2017 to 2022 Sun directed the Sciences Po–Fudan dual master’s program in Europe and Asia in International Affairs. A related visit was the Institute for Peace Research and Security Policy at the University of Hamburg in 2019, and the DAAD Strategic Partnerships China Fund project “Australia, China, EU: Relations Dogged by Controversies.”

Article entry: sunfanglu.github.io/publication/2021-populism-sino-european. Report entry: Compass Plan report.
