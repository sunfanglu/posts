Methods

Data mining and political analysis

2024 · Fanglu Sun

Sun, Fanglu. 2024. “Data Mining and Political Analysis.” In Cai Zuo (Ed.), Research Methods for Political Science. Fudan University Press.

Quantitative and big data methods are one of the four research areas listed on the academic homepage. The chapter belongs to that line of work, together with the book project “Data Mining and Analysis in Political Science: Theory, Practices, and Cases” and the earlier co-translation of John Gerring, Case Study Research: Principles and Practices (Chongqing Press, 2018).

Related support

  • Fund for Textbook Writing, Fudan University, 2019–2021. Data Mining and Analysis in Political Science.
  • Start-up Research Fund for Youth Faculty, Fudan University, 2017. Applications of Data Mining in Political Science Research.
  • Tools listed on the CV: R, Stata, Mathematica, Python, and ArcGIS.

A related invited talk is “Applications of Text Mining in Political Science Research,” Nankai University, 2017, and “Data for Peace: Early Warning System for Conflict Prevention and Management,” Kobe University, 2018.
