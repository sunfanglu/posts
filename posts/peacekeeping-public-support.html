Gauging Chinese public support for China’s role in peacekeeping

2019 · Fanglu Sun, with Songying Fang

Sun, Fanglu, with Songying Fang. 2019. “Gauging Chinese Public Support for China’s Role in Peacekeeping.” Chinese Journal of International Politics 12(2): 179–201.

The paper is listed as SSCI. It is part of a cluster on China’s participation in United Nations peacekeeping. The companion article is Sun, Fanglu, with Fang et al. 2018. “China’s Evolving Motivations and Goals in UN Peacekeeping Participation.” International Journal 73(3): 464–473.

Related talks

  • “Gauging Chinese Public Support for China’s Role in Peacekeeping.” Nankai University, 2017; and the Forum on Re-shaping World Order and China’s New Role, Nankai University, 2017.
  • “China’s Participation in UN Peacekeeping Operations: Motivations and Patterns.” Sciences Po, 2017.
  • “Morality, Responsibility, Legitimacy and Interests: A Comparative Investigation of Chinese Humanitarianism in International Peacekeeping.” Fudan Institute for Advanced Study, 2020.
  • “Gauging Chinese Public Support for China’s Role in Peacekeeping.” Workshop on Peacekeeping, Lanzhou University, 2022.
