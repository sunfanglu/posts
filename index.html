Hi, I'm Fanglu Sun.
孙芳露

Deputy Head and Assistant Professor in the Department of International Studies at Xi’an Jiaotong-Liverpool University. An international relations scholar, she uses quantitative and data-driven methods to study Chinese foreign policy and the role of the Global South in international organizations.

Welcome to my blog. Join me as I use data to explore how the world works and what the evidence can tell us about international politics.
